Privacy Policy

Barbados Income Tax Calculator

Effective date: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Conkie Labs ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in the Barbados Income Tax Calculator Android application (the "App"). The App helps users estimate Barbados PAYE, National Insurance and Social Security Service (NISSS) contributions, and take-home pay.

1. Information you enter

Salary amounts and other calculation inputs are processed locally on your device to produce estimates. The App does not require an account and does not send these inputs to Conkie Labs or to a server.

2. Information stored on your device

The App saves your preference for using NISSS earnings rounding in local application storage so the option is remembered between sessions. This setting is not transmitted to Conkie Labs.

3. Data collection and sharing

In the current version, the App does not collect or transmit personal information, salary inputs, or usage analytics to Conkie Labs. It does not include advertising SDKs and does not share personal data with advertisers or other third parties.

4. External links

The App includes a link to official NISSS guidance. Opening that link launches an external website or browser, which may handle information under its own privacy policy. Conkie Labs does not control those websites.

5. Security, retention, and deletion

Calculations take place on your device. The local rounding preference is stored in the App's private storage, rather than being uploaded to a Conkie Labs service. Because the App does not maintain user accounts or server-side personal data, there is no Conkie Labs account data to request for deletion. You can delete the locally stored preference by clearing the App's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the App.

6. Children's privacy

The App is designed as a general tax-estimation utility and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's functionality or data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. If future versions add features such as advertising or analytics, this policy will be updated to describe those practices.

8. Contact

For questions or privacy-related requests, contact Conkie Labs at conkielabs@gmail.com.
